Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 Asteroids Deluxe: Cyber Space Warfare

1. Overview

Welcome to Asteroids Deluxe: Cyber Space Warfare. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user data, game storage, and your privacy rights when playing our game.

Core Privacy Guarantee: Asteroids Deluxe runs entirely in your web browser. We do not require accounts, we do not collect personal identities, and we do not sell your personal data.

2. Information We Store Locally

To provide a smooth gaming experience without requiring a server login, the game uses your device's browser Local Storage solely for gameplay continuity:

  • High Scores & Combat Stats: Saved locally on your device to track your best runs and achievements.
  • Hangar & Upgrades: Crystals collected and unlocked starfighter configurations.
  • Audio & Settings: Volume preferences and sound toggle states.

This data stays on your local device. It is never transmitted to any external marketing servers.

3. Third-Party Services & Assets

The game loads static assets and libraries over secure Content Delivery Networks (CDNs) to ensure fast loading times:

  • Google Fonts: For game typography. Google may log standard IP request details to serve font files.
  • cdnjs / FontAwesome: For visual icons used in the game interface.

We do not embed third-party advertising trackers or invasive analytics SDKs into the gameplay.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our game is designed to be safe for players of all ages. In accordance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international standards, we do not knowingly solicit, collect, or process personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

5. Data Control & Deletion

Because all gameplay data is stored in your web browser's local cache, you maintain total control over your data. You can delete all scores, progress, and saved settings at any time by clearing your browser's site data or cookies for this domain.

6. Developer Contact & Inquiries

We may update this policy periodically to reflect game updates or regulatory changes. Continued play indicates acceptance of any revisions.
